not prose. The most important comes first; if space runs out, cut from the
bottom, never the top.

   reviewer not yet weighed, a `test-fast` result not yet read, a question to

   checked" is another. Never carry a claim as checked.
4. The review — each BLOCKER and FIX still open, and what closes it.
5. Decided — what the operator accepted and what they turned down, and why. A
   rejected idea that is not written here comes back.
